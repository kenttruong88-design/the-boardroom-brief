# 51 Layoff Announcements in a Week, 22 Mention a Number

A layoff tracker run by MetaIntro logged 51 announcements from 50 companies between 11 and 17 November, up from 48 the week before. The tracker counts layoffs, closures and return-to-office mandates in the same ledger, which is either a category error or an act of unusual honesty. Only 22 of the 51 entries gave a job count, and those that did added up to at least 6,079 jobs.

This column has done the arithmetic that the announcements declined to do. The 22 disclosed figures average out at about 276 jobs each, which means that for the other 29 announcements the number of affected employees is unknown. Corporate communications departments will describe this as discretion. The employees will describe it with a different noun.

## The disclosed

Among those that gave figures, Legal & General said on 16 November that it would cut 1,000 jobs in the UK by mid-2027, according to the tracker. Dentsu disclosed layoffs of 3,400 in the first nine months of 2026, alongside a lower annual outlook. Upwork, in an SEC filing on 12 November, indicated a total workforce reduction of about 37%, including an earlier cut of 24%. The marketplace for freelance work has, it seems, found a way to reduce its own headcount to the level of a freelancer's interest in a long-term contract.

AstraZeneca was reported on 16 November to be cutting about 600 US sales jobs, an estimate attributed by the tracker to Bloomberg and linked to a $1.5bn savings plan. PVH, the apparel company, reported in an 8-K filing on 12 November that about 4% of staff would go, and Athenahealth said it would reduce 4% of positions worldwide. Amazon, according to the tracker, is closing 72 Amazon Fresh and Go stores, affecting about 400 jobs in Washington state.

## The undisclosed

Fewer particulars arrived with other entries. The tracker recorded layoffs at Polymarket and a cut to its contractors, with no figure given. It recorded that Fortis Games would reduce up to 440 jobs, with staff saying Las Vegas Sands had ended funding. Elsewhere on the list Ferrara's Chicago plant will close in April 2027 with 154 layoffs, a delivery contractor in Manhattan will end about 200 jobs on 18 November, and Hello Fresh filed a WARN notice for 374 workers in Swedesboro, New Jersey.

Health and public employers appear as well: Presbyterian Healthcare with 135 jobs, the City of Toledo with 89, IHH Healthcare with about 380 in an estimate, Centene with 69 in Hawaii from 4 January, and Saddle Creek with 150 in Groveport, Ohio. The tracker counted two state WARN filings in the week, covering 524 workers. Athenahealth's 4% and PVH's 4% are each expressed as a share of staff, a unit of measure that has the advantage of requiring no headcount. The long tail is long. Faurecia Clean Mobility will close a plant in Franklin, Ohio, affecting 180 workers in five phases from 8 January 2027, and Kanes Foods, which employed 662 people, entered administration. Receivers are closing 19 of David Jones's 38 stores, Innoviz said it would cut about 20% of staff in the fourth quarter, and Vestas, Umicore, F-Secure and Cardlytics each appear with their own, smaller figures. Several entries describe reductions over long horizons: Legal & General's run to mid-2027, Dentsu's 3,400 covers nine months, and WE Soda's 350 are spread over two years. Whatever the tracker's total measures, it is not a week's dismissals.

The tracker is an aggregator and its entries should be read as company statements, filings and press reports rather than audited totals; two entries are marked as reported but not confirmed by the employer. That caveat applies to the numbers. It does not apply to the vocabulary, which is unaudited by design.

## A note on method

It is possible to construct a rough guide to corporate candour. A layoff announced with a figure, a date and a filing reference is an announcement. A layoff announced with a percentage is a hint. A layoff announced with a "savings plan" and an "outlook" is a mood.

Fifty-one such moods, hints and announcements were recorded last week. The employees affected will no doubt be reassured to learn that, for 29 of them, the tracker could find no one willing to say how many of them there were.
