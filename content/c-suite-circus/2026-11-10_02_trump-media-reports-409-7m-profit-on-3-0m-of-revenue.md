# Trump Media Reports $409.7M Profit on $3.0M of Revenue

Trump Media reported third-quarter net income of $409.7 million on Monday, on revenue of $3.0 million, according to the transcript of its earnings call published by Investing.com. Interim chief executive Kevin McGurn told listeners that revenue "grew 205% year-over-year to $3 million, and 78% sequentially". The profit, by contrast, was explained by chief financial officer Phillip Juhan, who said the $378.1 million reported gain on digital assets "largely reflects the 42% increase in the price of Bitcoin".

## Performance review: Q3 2026

**Revenue:** Growing quickly from a very small base. Three million dollars is up 205% on the year and 78% on the quarter, and is also a figure that rounds to nothing against total assets of $2.5 billion. A forensic reading of the percentage is that it describes a trajectory. A forensic reading of the dollar sum is that it describes a lemonade stand with a very large brokerage account.

**Profitability:** Wholly dependent on a colleague. Operating results excluding digital-asset gains and losses were considerably less festive: adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $12.3 million, an improvement on the $35.1 million loss of the second quarter. Operating expenses excluding digital assets fell to $26.5 million, down 45% on the quarter and 37% on the year. Legal expenses dropped to $2.4 million from more than $20 million in the second quarter, a line item whose decline requires no further commentary.

**Treasury management:** Bold. The company held 15,649 Bitcoin at September 30 and bought 4,359 during the quarter, according to the call's later sections. The price of Bitcoin rose 42% over those three months, and the balance sheet rose with it. It is worth stating plainly that this is exposure, not operations. The same mechanism that produced a $378.1 million gain in a rising quarter produces the opposite in a falling one, and the company's interim chief executive was not asked, in the portions of the transcript available, to describe the plan for that.

**Capital structure:** Interesting. The company has about $1 billion of convertible notes due on November 30. It has a $400 million share repurchase authorisation, of which $40 million has been used and none in the third quarter. Management said it does not want to issue additional equity. The reader is invited to reconcile the three sentences, as management presumably has.

For scale, financial assets of $2.3 billion make up the bulk of the company's $2.5 billion in total assets. The business that gave the company its name is therefore a rounding item on a balance sheet that is mostly a portfolio. Scott Glabe, the general counsel, also joined the call, which is a reasonable allocation of attendance given the merger and the notes.

**Strategy:** Pending. The company is merging with TAE Technologies. The combined business would have about 600 million fully diluted shares, roughly double the current count, and a special meeting of shareholders is set for December 14. The S-4 registration statement has been declared effective, and the transcript says the deal is expected to close by the end of the year. Separately, the company has discontinued its token distribution programme and said it will offer shareholder benefits instead, which is the corporate equivalent of replacing a loyalty card with a different loyalty card.

## The reviewer's note

It is entirely legitimate for a company to hold a volatile asset, to report its movements under the relevant accounting rules, and to describe the result in its earnings call. It is also reasonable for an observer to place the numbers side by side. A profit that is more than 130 times revenue is not a measure of how the business performed. It is a measure of how the market performed, and the business was present at the time.

The cost discipline is real. Operating expenses are down, legal bills are down and the loss at the adjusted EBITDA level has narrowed by nearly two thirds. If Mr McGurn can sustain those improvements while revenue continues to grow from $3 million, that would be a substantive result worth reporting in its own right. It would also be a considerably smaller headline.

Overall rating: meets expectations on cost control, exceeds on market luck, and requires a development plan for what happens when the colleague who produced the quarter's profit takes a quarter off.
