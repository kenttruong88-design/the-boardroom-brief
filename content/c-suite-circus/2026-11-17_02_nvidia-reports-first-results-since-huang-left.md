# Nvidia Reports First Results Since Huang Left, Delivered by the CFO

Nvidia will report fiscal third-quarter results on Tuesday, November 17, at about 1:20 p.m. Pacific time, with a call at 2 p.m., the company said in its November 12 announcement of Jensen Huang's resignation. It is the first report since Huang left as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately. The person delivering it is Colette Kress, the chief financial officer, who agreed to serve as interim chief executive while remaining chief financial officer.

## Exhibit A: the org chart

In the language of corporate governance, this is known as concentration of responsibility. In the language of ordinary employment, it is a person who has been asked to do two senior jobs at once, one of which was vacated with no notice. Nvidia said Kress joined the company in September 2013 and spent nearly 25 years in finance at technology companies including Cisco and Microsoft before that.

The board has begun a search for a permanent chief executive and will consider internal and external candidates, according to the company. It also named Harvey C. Jones, a technology executive and venture investor who was previously chief executive and executive chairman of Synopsys, as the first-ever chair of its board. Jones said the board has full confidence in Kress and the leadership team and expects operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap to continue without interruption.

Reuters, citing the Financial Times, reported that Huang is considering a return as chief executive. Nvidia has announced nothing of the kind, and the company's own statement describes the search for a successor as under way. A board that is simultaneously recruiting a replacement and hearing that the predecessor might return has a scheduling problem that no governance code anticipates.

## Exhibit B: the shareholder reaction

The market has already delivered its interim assessment. According to the Associated Press, Nvidia fell 4.1% on Monday and has lost $735 billion in market value since the disclosures around Huang's exit. Blockhead reported that the shares fell 12.75% last week to $162.85, about 32% below the record close of October 6. Reuters said Friday ended Nvidia's longest weekly losing streak since October 2012.

An executive team would ordinarily regard a 32% drawdown from peak as a significant event. Nvidia is not an ordinary company: Schwab said it holds about 80% of the market for advanced AI chips, and analysts expect revenue and earnings per share to be up about 90% from a year ago. Blockhead cited analyst estimates of roughly $109 billion in quarterly revenue. The damage was not confined to Nvidia, either. CoreWeave, Dell and HP each fell about 5% in premarket trading on Monday, according to Reuters, which suggests investors are treating the leadership question as a sector-wide input and not a company-specific one. Companies with those numbers usually receive congratulations, not a leadership review, and the divergence tells you what investors are really pricing.

## Exhibit C: what the call needs

The first test is simple: guidance. A new interim leader has an incentive to set the bar at a height she can clear, and investors know it. The second test is continuity. In the November 12 announcement, Kress referred to the extraordinary foundation Huang created over 33 years, a sentence that is accurate and also the one every successor says. Analysts will listen for any evidence of a change in customer commitments, supply allocation or pricing.

The third test is external. The AP reported that the Trump administration has issued rules restricting remote access to advanced US AI chips and servers, and Schwab, citing Bloomberg, said licences will be required to re-export advanced AI chips to Brazil, Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand. Those constraints land on a company that has just lost its public face and is now asking investors to focus on the roadmap.

The call has one structural oddity. Ordinarily the chief executive offers the strategy and the chief financial officer explains the numbers, so that each can be questioned on the other's account. On Tuesday afternoon those roles will be merged, and any question that begins "can you reconcile your strategy with your forecast" will be answered by the person who oversaw both.

## Performance review

Nvidia has a record that most chief executives would envy and an organisational chart that most auditors would query. The quarter is almost certainly strong. The question is whether the seat is steady, and for that, there is no line item on the income statement.
